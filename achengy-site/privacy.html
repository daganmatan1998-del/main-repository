Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This site is operated by the operator of this site. This page explains what information the site handles.

What we collect

The site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you send an enquiry, the name, email address and message you type are used only to reply to you.

Storage in your browser

  • achengy-no-motion (local storage) remembers if you chose to stop animations.
  • achengy-seen and achengy-wipe (session storage) stop the intro screen repeating and smooth page transitions. They are cleared when you close the tab.

Enquiries

Depending on how the site is set up, an enquiry is sent to a form service, or opens in your WhatsApp, email or Instagram app with your message prepared. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any information you have sent us, using the contact details on this site.
