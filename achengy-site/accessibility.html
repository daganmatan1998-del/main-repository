Accessibility

Last updated: October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site.

What we do

  • The whole site works without JavaScript and without animation.
  • Animations stop if your device asks for reduced motion, and the "Stop animations" button in the footer turns them off for good.
  • There is a skip link, visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields and text alternatives for images.
  • Text and cyan accents are chosen for strong contrast against the dark background.
  • Layouts adapt from phone to desktop.

Standard

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Some parts may not yet fully meet it.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, please send a message through the enquiry form and tell us what happened, and we will work to fix it.
