3. Who we share it with

We share only what each provider needs, under contracts requiring them to protect it:

  • Payment provider () — processes your payment on its own secure page.
  • Production and fulfilment partners — receive your name, delivery address, phone and order contents to print and ship your items.
  • Carriers and customs brokers — receive delivery details to deliver your parcel and clear customs.
  • Hosting, email and image-delivery providers — operate the site and send our emails.
  • Authorities — where required by law, such as tax or customs authorities.

We never sell your personal data and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by California law.

4. International transfers

Because our providers and fulfilment partners operate in several countries, including the United States and the European Union, your data may be transferred outside Israel and outside your country. We transfer data only in line with the Protection of Privacy (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001 and, where relevant, using appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: 7 years, as required by Israeli tax and accounting law.
  • Customer-service correspondence: up to 3 years after the last contact.
  • Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe, then a suppression record so we do not email you again.
  • Data stored on your device (bag, saved pieces, choices): until you clear it from your browser.

6. Security

We apply the security measures required by the Data Security Regulations, including access control, encryption in transit (HTTPS) and limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission is perfectly secure; if a security incident affects your data, we will notify you and the regulator where the law requires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data, where we are not required to keep it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • opt out of direct marketing — every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, as required by section 30A of the Israeli Communications Law (anti-spam).

Send requests to . We will verify your identity and reply within 30 days (or sooner where the law requires). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or to the data-protection authority of your country.

We will post any update to this policy here with a new effective date, and tell you by email if a change materially affects you.
