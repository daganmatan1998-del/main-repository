Our standard

This site was designed and built to conform with Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 at level AA, as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013. We also follow WCAG 2.1 AA where we can.

What we have done

  • The whole store can be used with a keyboard: a “Skip to content” link, visible focus outlines, and dialogs (bag, search, quick view, cookie choices) that trap focus and close with Escape.
  • Semantic structure with landmarks and headings, labelled form fields, error messages announced to screen readers, and text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Colour and size options use proper radio-button semantics and can be changed with the arrow keys.
  • Text contrast meets AA ratios; text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content; layouts adapt to phones, tablets and desktops.
  • All motion respects your system “reduce motion” setting: smooth scrolling, scroll-driven scenes and the opening animation are switched off and replaced by static layouts.
  • No content flashes more than three times per second, and nothing moves automatically for more than five seconds without your scrolling.

Known limitations

Some decorative campaign images have empty text alternatives by design. The payment page is provided by our payment processor and is outside our direct control, though it is designed to be accessible. If you find any barrier, please tell us — we will fix it, and help you complete your order in the meantime.
