What we use

This site stores a small amount of information in your browser using local storage (which works like cookies). Today we use only strictly necessary and preference storage. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking cookies; if we add them in the future, they will stay switched off until you allow them in .

Third parties

Product and campaign images are delivered from a content-delivery network, which receives your IP address and browser details in order to send the files, as any web server does. Our fonts and animation libraries are hosted on our own site. When you pay, our payment provider () sets its own strictly necessary cookies on its checkout page to process payment and prevent fraud; these are governed by its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can change your choices at any time from in the footer of every page. You can also clear site data in your browser settings; this empties your bag and saved pieces. Blocking strictly necessary storage will prevent the bag and checkout from working.
