Production time

Because each item is printed for you, orders enter production after payment and are ready to ship in (Israeli and production-country public holidays excluded). Orders with several items may ship in separate parcels from different facilities at no extra cost to you.

Methods, times and rates

Complimentary standard shipping applies automatically when the order subtotal, after any discounts, reaches . Delivery times are estimates that begin once your order has shipped; they are not guaranteed and can be affected by customs and carrier conditions.

Duties & import taxes

    Duties and taxes are set by the destination country and collected by the carrier or customs authority, not by VANTÉ. If you refuse to pay them, the parcel is returned to us; we will refund the price of the goods minus the original shipping charge and any return costs we are charged by the carrier. Under Israeli import rules, personal parcels above the applicable value threshold are subject to VAT and, in some cases, customs duty — please check before ordering.

    Tracking

    When your order ships you will receive an email with the carrier and tracking link. Tracking may take up to 48 hours to show movement.

    Address accuracy

    Please make sure your address and phone number are complete and correct — couriers in Israel and the Gulf contact recipients by phone. If a parcel is returned because the address was incomplete or the parcel was not collected, we can reship it once the new shipping charge is paid, or refund the goods minus shipping.

    Late, lost or damaged parcels

    If your order has not arrived within 10 business days after the end of the estimated delivery window, contact and we will open a carrier investigation. If the parcel is confirmed lost, we will reship your order or refund it in full, as you prefer. If an item arrives damaged, misprinted or incorrect, send us a photo within days of delivery and we will replace or refund it at our cost — you will not need to return it in most cases.
