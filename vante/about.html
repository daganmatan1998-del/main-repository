About the house

Made to
be you.

VANTÉ is a quiet house. We draw statues, wings and mountains, and set them on heavyweight cotton for people who would rather be understood than noticed.

i.

The sketchbook

It started in Israel, with a sketchbook carried into galleries and cast collections. Backs of statues, mostly — figures turned toward the wall, strength shown without asking for attention. Those drawings became Marble & Ink, the first chapter of the house.

ii.

One line of gold

The Celestial line studies are drawn in a single stroke — Pegasus, a falling star, a cherub in flight — printed in champagne on black and taupe on bone. Two colours only. We would rather perfect black and white than dilute them.

iii.

Made to order

Nothing waits in a warehouse. When you order, your piece is printed by our production partners on 240–400 gsm cotton, inspected and shipped directly to you — to the United States, Israel and the Gulf. No overstock, no end-of-season landfill, and a price that reflects the garment rather than the markdown.

What we hold to

Black & white

Every design exists in both, and only both — for now. New colours arrive only when they deserve to.

Honest prices

No inflated “was” prices and no countdown timers. The price you see is the price, and the drop numbers are real.

Printed to order

Produced when you order it, which means a few extra days — and nothing made that nobody wanted.
