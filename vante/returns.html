Cancelling an order

Before production

You may cancel any order free of charge until it enters production — usually within a few hours of payment. Email with your order number as soon as possible; if production has not started, we refund the full amount, including shipping.

Your statutory right to cancel (Israel)

Under section 14C of the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, you may cancel a distance purchase within 14 days from the day you receive the goods or the day you receive the written disclosure document for the transaction, whichever is later. To cancel, notify us in writing at (or using the contact form), including your name, ID number where required, and your order number.

When you cancel under this right we refund the price you paid within 14 days of receiving your cancellation notice, minus a cancellation fee of 5% of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower, and you return the goods to us. If you cancel because of a defect, a non-conformity, late delivery or a breach by us, no cancellation fee applies and we cover return costs.

Consumers who are persons with disabilities, senior citizens (65 and over) or new immigrants (within five years of immigration) have an extended cancellation period of four months where the transaction included a conversation with us (including by phone or chat), as set out in the law.

Outside Israel

If you live outside Israel, you also benefit from any mandatory cancellation or return rights of your country of residence. Our own policy below applies to everyone.

Returns within days

In addition to your statutory rights, you may return any unworn, unwashed item with its original labels within days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Please note:

  • Items must be returned in the condition you received them. We may reduce the refund for items that show wear, washing, odours or damage.
  • Because every piece is made to order, return shipping is paid by you unless the item is faulty or incorrect. Original shipping charges are refunded only for faulty or incorrect items, or when you cancel the entire order under your statutory right where the law requires it.
  • We recommend a tracked service — we cannot refund items that do not reach us.
  • Limited-edition items may be returned under these same rules.

How to return

  1. Email with your order number, the items you are returning and the reason (optional, but it helps us).
  2. We reply within one business day with the return address for your region. Do not send items to our business address without a return authorisation — returns are processed by our fulfilment partners.
  3. Pack the items securely, include your order number, and send them within 14 days of our reply.

Refunds

Refunds are made to your original payment method within 14 days after we receive and inspect the returned items (or, for statutory cancellations, within the period the law requires). Your bank may take a further 5–10 business days to show the credit. Duties and import taxes paid to a customs authority are not collected by us and cannot be refunded by us; you may be able to reclaim them from the carrier or customs authority.

Exchanges

As each piece is made to order, the fastest way to exchange is to return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If your item is defective, damaged in transit, misprinted, or not what you ordered, contact us within days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or a full refund including shipping, at your choice and at our cost. This does not affect your statutory rights regarding defective goods.
